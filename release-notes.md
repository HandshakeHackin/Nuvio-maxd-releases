Nuvio Max'd 0.5.18 improves playback and subtitles:

- Updated audio timing and synchronized recovery after seeking or decoder failure.
- Surround PCM audio, with stereo fallback and a Settings toggle.
- Styled ASS/SSA and bitmap subtitles, including PGS tracks.
- Bundled CJK and Thai fonts with improved subtitle shaping.
- Additional HDR format checks, matching pixel packing and clearer errors.
- Reusable packet storage and bounded audio buffering.

The app shows a simple changelog after updating; reopen it in Settings → What's new.

Download PPSA99288.zip for folder installation or NuvioMaxd-0.5.18-native.ffpfsc for image installation.
Matching source, validation results and SHA256SUMS are included.
