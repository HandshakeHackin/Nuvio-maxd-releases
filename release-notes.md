Nuvio Max’d 0.5.16 — playback and interface refresh

- Improved native frame timing to avoid unnecessary drops from small scheduling delays.
- Moved logging into the background to reduce playback interruptions.
- Updated graphics libraries and build tools, including a graphics-buffer lifetime fix.
- Improved wrapping for long words and languages without spaces.
- Added **Calmer backgrounds** in Settings to keep decorative backgrounds still.
- Updated the display declaration for older PS5 firmware compatibility.

EVO Player 0.12.0 remains available in Settings → Playback engine. Native playback remains the default. Existing settings and accounts are preserved.

Experimental: build and automated checks are verified; motion smoothness and native HDR acceptance still need console confirmation.
