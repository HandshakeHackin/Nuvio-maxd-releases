Nuvio Max'd 0.5.27 — Settings, lighting and playback improvements

- Find settings more easily in eight categories, with Cross to open and Circle to go back.
- Enjoy a slow teal → white → pink/purple controller lightbar fade. Reduced Motion keeps it steady.
- Enable or disable console light sync in Appearance & Lighting. The choice is saved; supported consoles approximate the colors using their available lighting channels.
- Improve playback frame timing using completed display flips.
- Improve PCM audio compatibility and stereo downmixing, with clearer playback diagnostics.
- Retain the experimental HDR output improvements and previous startup fixes.

Close Nuvio before replacing its image or app folder. Keep your app data to retain login and preferences.

This is an experimental release. Host checks, native build and package verification passed; console lighting compatibility and end-to-end playback still need PS5 testing. Console colors are approximate, and unexpected process termination cannot guarantee restoration. EVO green-picture/allocation issues and optical bitstream compatibility are not claimed fixed. For audio comparison, start with Native playback, PCM and Surround Sound off.

Matching source, validation report and SHA256SUMS are included. Delivery branch: builds/v0.5.27.
