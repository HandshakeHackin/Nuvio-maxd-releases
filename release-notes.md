Nuvio Max’d 0.5.17 — playback controls and accessibility

- A simple “What’s new” screen on first launch and after updates; reopen it from Settings.
- Subtitle font, colour, outline/shadow and background opacity, with a live preview in Settings.
- Adjustable D-pad and L1/R1 seek intervals.
- Adjustable next-episode countdown during credits and when an episode ends.
- Reduced-motion and higher-contrast interface options.
- Playback, frame-timing and crash diagnostics, with one previous run retained. New diagnostic snapshots omit stream URLs and account credentials.

Existing defaults, accounts and watch progress are preserved. EVO Player 0.12.0 remains optional; native playback remains the default.

Experimental: automated checks and package contents are verified. Native HDR acceptance and remaining motion judder still need console testing.
