Noto Sans CJK SC Regular, SIL OFL 1.1.

Prepared from https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk at commit
f8d157532fbfaeda587e826d4cd5b21a49186f7c,
Sans/Variable/TTF/NotoSansCJKsc-VF.ttf.

Converted to static weight 400 with fonttools 4.61.1 using
native-app/tools/prepare_cjk_font.py. The TrueType quadratic outlines work
with both libass/FreeType and the UI signed-distance-field renderer.
Source and output hashes are recorded in native-app/dependencies.json.
The complete OFL license is in NotoSansCJK-LICENSE.txt.
