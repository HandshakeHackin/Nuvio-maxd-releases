Nuvio Subtitle Fallback Regular

A modified font combining the bundled Noto Sans, Noto Sans Thai and Inter
regular faces. It retains all 4,021 input Unicode code points, including
Thai marks and Latin, Greek and Cyrillic text. Outlines are normalized to
1000 units per em, resolution-specific hinting is removed, layout tables are
merged and the family/PostScript names are changed. Original bundled faces
are unchanged. No console/system font is included.
