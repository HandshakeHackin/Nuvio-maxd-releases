Nuvio Max'd 1.0.0.18 — menu languages

Fully close the app before installation. Preserve saved data and your previous
installer; do not overwrite a mounted FFPFSC. Confirm the version shows 1.0.0.18.

1. In Settings > App & Updates > Language select a language by its native name:
   English, Deutsch, Español, Français, العربية, 简体中文, 日本語 or 한국어.
   Fully close and restart. Return to the picker and confirm the saved choice.
2. Check home navigation, setting categories/values, sign-in, profile PIN,
   playback tracks/controls, cast/person pages and USB import/cancellation.
   The six new catalogs cover 425 core messages; remaining text can use English.
3. Check Arabic connected letters and mixed Latin codes/numbers. Check Chinese,
   Japanese kana/kanji and Korean Hangul for missing boxes or clipped labels.
   The screen layout and controller order are unchanged.
4. Search settings in the selected language. Try accents with/without marks,
   Arabic with/without vowel marks, and Chinese/Japanese/Korean text.
5. Switch profiles, restart and switch back. The app's language stays selected;
   each profile's sources and saved USB copies must stay separate.
6. Confirm audio/subtitle preferences remain independent. Check playback and
   USB import, cancellation and replacement. Never share a real playlist.
7. Switch back to English and restart. Report exact screen/action, firmware,
   install type, and corrected wording if needed. No account data, credentials,
   private URLs or playlist contents are needed for translation feedback.

Host checks validate coverage, formatting, persistence, fallback, fonts, shaping
and rendering. Console behavior and translation quality still need user feedback.
