Nuvio Max'd 1.0.0.12 experimental — issue #10 console checklist

Close the app and install the matching ZIP folder or native image. Preserve user data and keep the previous working installer. Do not replace a mounted image.

1. Confirm App updates shows 1.0.0.12. Check profile, saved IPTV source, artwork and resume survived the update.
2. While watching a title with several loaded sources, press Up / Options, then Square. Choose another source. Verify the same title resumes near the prior position; repeat, cancel and stop during loading.
3. With built-in torrents enabled and an active multi-video torrent, choose another torrent file. Check per-file progress and cancel the lookup/picker. This is separate from changing providers for the same title.
4. Open Subtitle appearance in playback options. Change size, font, colour, effect and background, close the menu, then restart the app and check persistence. Bitmap subtitles cannot be restyled; authored ASS/VLC may retain their own styling.
5. With Bluetooth headphones, try -/+0.10 s and -/+0.20 s audio delay, then reset. Positive delays sound; confirm direction and saved settings. Existing L2/R2 adjustments in the track menu are 0.05 s.
6. Show stream controls. Check resolution, decoder and rate details with Native and VLC. Native reports container bitrate when provided; VLC reports measured input rate, not encoded file bitrate or peer download speed. Missing data should say unavailable.
7. Live TV > Sources & filters > Guide layout enables the time grid. Try channel up/down, time left/right, groups, search, favorites and channel pages. Cross tunes live, including channels with no listings. Future cells are listings, not recording or catch-up. Switch back to channel tiles and restart to check the preference.
8. Optional: Settings > Sports Hub is off by default. Enable it, choose sports/leagues/teams/region, then check schedules and available channel candidates against your own Live TV source. Disable and confirm selections are retained.

Report version, PS5 firmware, player (Native/EVO/VLC), folder/image install, what passed and exact steps/errors. Remove provider credentials, tokens and private stream URLs from logs. Console acceptance remains pending.
