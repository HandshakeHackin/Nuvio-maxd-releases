Nuvio Max'd 1.0.0.17 — language and people browsing

Fully close the app before installing. Keep saved data and the previous installer;
do not overwrite a mounted FFPFSC. Confirm the app shows 1.0.0.17.

German interface (request in issue #20):
1. Open Settings > App & Updates > Language, choose Deutsch, then fully close
   and restart Max'd. Look for Einstellungen > App und Updates > Sprache.
2. Check navigation, settings, sign-in/profile PIN screens, playback menus and
   Live TV/USB prompts. Search settings using UNTERTITEL, GRÖSSE or GROSSE.
3. Switch profiles and restart again. The app language should remain selected;
   audio/subtitle preferences, addon content and each profile's sources stay
   independent. Choose English and restart to switch back.
4. Check USB import/cancellation in German. Existing private playlist copies
   and the working import behavior from 1.0.0.16 must remain available.

Cast & crew / person pages (issue #21):
1. Open a movie or series and press R2. Browse cast/director/creator photos with
   Left/Right and select a person with Cross. R2 or Circle leaves the cast row.
2. Check the person's photo, biography, birth date/place and known-for field.
   Missing data uses a placeholder; an empty German biography falls back to English.
3. Browse Movies and TV shows with Up/Down; use Left/Right within a row, including
   the last cards. Cross opens a title through this profile's configured addons.
   A title your addons do not support should give a message rather than a guess.
4. Use Circle to return through person/title pages. Check the cast/person focus,
   repeated browsing, offline/error Retry, and leaving while details are loading.
   Existing playback, source selection and episode navigation must still work.
5. Switch profiles during loading. Results and navigation from the old profile
   must not appear in the new one. Browsing alone should not mark titles watched.

Bounds: 80 cast/crew people; 256 movie and 256 TV credits per person; eight
navigation snapshots; 2 MiB metadata replies. Image requests use the existing
bounded artwork cache. Some titles have no TMDB mapping/photos or no matching
configured addon. Private playlists, account files, credentials and stream URLs
are not needed for feedback. Report the exact screen/action and firmware/install
type; review any shared logs for private data.
