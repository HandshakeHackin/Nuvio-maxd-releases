# Nuvio Max'd 1.0.0.26 — age ratings

- Movie and series details now show TMDB age certifications alongside year, runtime and genres.
- Choose from 32 countries in Settings → Account & Services → Age-rating country. The preference is saved per profile and included in configuration backups.
- The badge names its country. US ratings are used when the selected country's rating is missing; no badge appears when both are unavailable.
- Country names and settings are translated into all eight menu languages. Ratings are display only.

Close Max'd before updating and keep your app data. ZIP, native image, matching source and verification results are included.
